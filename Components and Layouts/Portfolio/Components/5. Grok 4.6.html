Money-weighted vs time-weighted

Both returns, the benchmark, and the outperformance, side by side. Timing of your contributions is the whole argument.

Medium
7.a Twin returns — period switch rewrites every figure

Your timing, and the market’s.

7.b Marker bars — benchmark is a tick, not a fourth bar

1-year, against an 8.1% benchmark tick.

Money-weighted
11.4%
Time-weighted
9.8%
Gold tick is the benchmark. Bars run to 15%.
Money-weighted cares when you added cash. Time-weighted pretends every day had the same stake. Outperformance here is time-weighted minus benchmark: +1.7pp. Money-weighted outperformance would be +3.3pp, and that flatters the timing.
7.c Plain-English formulae — the number is a stamp on the method

Money-weighted

The return that solves for your actual cashflows. A deposit the week before a rally counts for more. Yours is +11.4% over one year.

Time-weighted

Chain the periods between cashflows so timing drops out. This is the number to set next to a benchmark. Yours is +9.8% versus +8.1%.
