51.bEditorial masthead — serif headline, running description and a numbered fact column
Company profile · updated 8 April 2026

Arcadia Semiconductor Corporation

ARCS · NASDAQ · reporting in US dollars

Arcadia sits at the centre of the accelerated-computing build-out. Roughly half of group revenue now comes from data-centre silicon sold to a small number of very large buyers, with a smaller but far more profitable licensing business attached to its interconnect patents.

The company is fabless. It designs, tests and sells; two external foundries manufacture. That keeps capital intensity at around nine per cent of revenue but leaves the cost base exposed to wafer pricing negotiated every three years.
