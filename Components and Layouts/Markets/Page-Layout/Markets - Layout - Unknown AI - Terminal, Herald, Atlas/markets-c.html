Why it matters here

For a decade gold was simply the inverse of the ten-year real yield, and every allocation model treated it as such. Since 2024 the relationship has snapped: central banks have bought for eleven consecutive months and the metal now trades $366 above what rates justify.

If you still run a real-yield model on gold, your hedge is quietly broken — and the breakdown is a macro signal in its own right, because it says reserve diversification, not speculation, is setting the price.
